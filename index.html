HELP CENTER

SkillLink FAQ

Quick answers about learning, payments, certificates, referrals and platform access.

What is SkillLink?

SkillLink is a digital learning and practical-skills platform that connects courses, masterclasses, projects, assessments, certificates and eligible platform opportunities.

Who can use SkillLink?

Anyone who meets the applicable account and program requirements can create an account. Individual packages and activities may have their own eligibility requirements.

Can I learn on mobile?

Yes. The website is designed to work on modern mobile browsers as well as desktop screens.

How do certificates work?

For eligible courses, completion is followed by an assessment. A certificate can be generated when the required passing criteria are met.

How does referral tracking work?

Eligible partner referral links can associate a new registration with the referring partner. Commission is credited only after the applicable verification rules are satisfied.

Is every masterclass commissionable?

No. SkillLink's partner rules determine which activities qualify. The applicable bonus or commission is shown in the partner program rules.

How does withdrawal work?

Partners can request withdrawal when they meet the minimum amount and available-balance requirements. Requests are subject to the platform's approval process.

Where can I get support?

Use the Contact & Support section below or the support details configured by SkillLink in the platform settings.
