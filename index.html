QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I get after joining?

You get access to the learning content and platform features included with the package or masterclass you purchase.

Can I learn from my phone?

Yes. SkillLink is designed with a mobile-first experience so you can access your learning journey from supported phones and browsers.

Are these only video courses?

No. The platform is designed around structured learning plus practical projects, masterclasses and supporting platform features.

Is payment required?

SkillLink has paid packages and masterclasses. Always check the current price shown on the official checkout before paying.

How does the referral system work?

Eligible partner referral links can be associated with new registrations. Referral rewards are subject to SkillLink's verification and commission rules.

Where can I get support?

Use the support/contact options provided on the platform for account, payment or technical questions.
